3.5e encounter reminders

Attack roll: d20 + attack bonus vs. AC.

Saving throw: d20 + save bonus vs. effect DC.

Initiative: d20 + Dexterity modifier.

Encounter Level: Combine creature CRs using the 3.5e encounter-building rules; do not simply add CR values.

Treasure: Compare total encounter treasure against the appropriate 3.5e treasure guidelines and campaign wealth assumptions.

This tool provides prompts and estimates. It is not a substitute for the official 3.5e rules text, encounter tables, or full stat blocks.
